Collection of data analysis and data science projects developed in Python
and Google Colab, covering exploratory analysis, statistical modeling,
machine learning, and Monte Carlo simulations applied to real world datasets.

## Projects

### 1. Bike Sharing Demand Analysis
Seasonal and weekly demand analysis on UCI bike sharing dataset.
Includes Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Linear Regression and
Monte Carlo simulation for 2013 forecast.

### 2. Cryptocurrency Market Analysis – BTC, SOL, TRX
Historical price analysis, correlation study, buy signal indicators
and Monte Carlo price forecast to 2030 for Bitcoin, Solana and TRON.

### 3. Movie Data Analysis and Revenue Prediction
Genre correlation analysis, rating vs revenue study and Monte Carlo
simulation for box office revenue prediction using IMDB dataset.

### 4. COVID-19 Colombia – Mortality and Survival Analysis
Complete analysis of 6.3 million records covering mortality by sex
and age, survival threshold study, geographic distribution and
Random Forest risk prediction model.
